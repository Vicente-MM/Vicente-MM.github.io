3D Scanning and Reverse Engineering

Confidentiality Notice: Due to confidentiality, photos and videos cannot be shared. Any photos shown are of similar systems.

Overview

  • Led project to develop 3D scanning and reverse engineering capabilities for various components
  • Researched in-house and externally sourced 3D scanning options including photogrammetry, laser and structured light scanners
  • Reconstructed technical drawings and Siemens NX CAD models from scanned data

My Role

  • Liaised with Mercedes-Benz Australia engineering team to understand requirements and project scope
  • Communicated with suppliers to evaluate and select appropriate 3D scanning equipment
  • Took initiative to be trained in operating 3D scanners in order to better select the correct equipment
  • Organised logistics of onboarding supplier and arranging for the 3D scanning of initial trial parts. Wrote work instruction to document this process
  • Managed the entire 3D scanning and reverse engineering process from initial research to final implementation

Results & Impact

  • Established 3D scanning workflow for rapid transfer of scan data to CAD
  • Decreased measurement time compared to traditional methods by 5x
  • Enabled the reverse-engineering and continued manufacture of discontinued spare parts for key customers
  • Gained first-hand experience using 3D scanners and scan-to-CAD software
